DRAFT YSWS

Lost Signal is a Hack Club draft YSWS for people who want to build communication tools for the moments when the internet or cellular networks disappear. Teams will design, prototype, test, and share a working project that helps nearby people stay connected offline.

Your project can be hardware, software, or both. We want practical experiments such as local message networks, rescue radios, offline coordination tools, peer-to-peer apps, and low-power devices that remain useful in emergencies, remote places, or network outages.

LoRa messengers

Build a small LoRa node that sends text or sensor data across long distances without Wi-Fi or cellular service.

Mesh networks

Create a network where phones or computers relay messages to nearby devices until every part of a group can be reached.

Radio tools

Experiment with radio hardware, Morse, APRS, emergency alerts, or simple custom protocols for communication without infrastructure.

Offline chat

Make a local chat app or message board that connects devices directly over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, local servers, or peer discovery.

Bluetooth

Prototype a nearby-device tool that shares location, status, files, or messages when a normal connection is unavailable.

Your idea

Invent your own way to keep information moving. Projects can focus on rural access, disaster response, privacy, accessibility, or anything else you discover needs offline communication.

FAQ

What is a YSWS?

YSWS means You Ship, We Ship: build a project, ship it, and earn rewards after it is reviewed by Hack Club.

What are we building for Lost Signal?

Lost Signal is a draft YSWS about communication tools that work without the internet or cellular networks.

Can I work with a team or by myself?

Both are welcome. Teams should coordinate so everyone has a clear part in designing, building, documenting, or testing the project.

Do I need experience with radios or hardware?

No. Software projects, small prototypes, simulations, and beginner-friendly experiments are all valid starting points.
